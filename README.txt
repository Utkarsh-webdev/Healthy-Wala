HEALTHY WALA WEBSITE DEMO

Files:
- index.html: responsive demo website
- assets/: product catalogue crops and the new 3D hero artwork

Preview:
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Keep the assets folder in the same folder as index.html.
3. Open index.html in Chrome or another modern browser.

Features:
- Refreshed 3D-style hero illustration with vegetables, orange and salad
- Menu cards use the supplied product catalogue photos
- Category filters, combo cards, subscription options and contact section
- WhatsApp enquiry links with prefilled messages
- Google Fonts (DM Sans and Manrope); Lucide library is loaded for icon support

Important:
This is a static demo. It has no admin panel, order database, payment gateway or backend. WhatsApp buttons open prefilled messages; they do not submit orders automatically. Product photos are crops from the screenshots provided and may be lower quality than original product photography. Confirm prices, products, policies, contact details and branding with the business before publishing.
